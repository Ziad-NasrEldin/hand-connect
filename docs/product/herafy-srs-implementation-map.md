## High-Level Read

- The MVP is substantially built: discovery, approved profiles, chat/WhatsApp contact, contact-gated reviews, provider/admin flows, abuse reporting, and product boundaries exist.
- The biggest unfinished areas are **phone auth**, **ranking governance/configuration**, **Paymob/card renewal and automatic expiry jobs**, **banned-profile public hiding tests**, **analytics/event tracking**, and **moving remaining privileged admin/payment-like mutations fully server-side**.
- No audited feature group was completely absent, but many are only partially enforced or only tested in demo/plain test mode rather than Firebase emulator/integration mode.

3. Complete ranking governance: policy config, no-cap paid product rules, activity signal source, and broader tests.
4. Complete monetization model: Paymob card-on-file renewal, 250 EGP monthly area expansion, automatic paid-benefit expiry, and explicit promoted labels.
5. Add Firebase/e2e coverage for phone identifier login and email verification behavior.
6. Add remaining emulator coverage for paid expiry, banned/rejected/suspended search exclusions, approval-to-public visibility, and any callable paths not covered by current test doubles.
7. Add product-boundary regression tests to keep booking/payments/job assignment/certifications/multi-city out of v1.
