## Design Context

### Users

Hand Connect serves people in Cairo who need trusted home-service professionals quickly on mobile, plus providers and admins managing listings and leads. Customers browse in Arabic-first RTL contexts, often on phones, and need to compare providers, inspect credibility, and start direct contact without friction.

### Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, refined. The interface should feel warm and competent rather than loud, sharp, or intimidating. It should communicate that Hand Connect is a reliable connection layer, not a flashy operator.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the existing orange accent and Cairo-first identity, but move away from the current brutalist, high-contrast box treatment. Use a light theme with warm paper tones, softened edges, quieter borders, restrained depth, and cleaner mobile-first layouts. Preserve some assertive brand moments in hero/logo typography, while letting most app surfaces feel smoother and more breathable.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve recognition: keep the core brand accent and Arabic-first identity without preserving the harsh chrome.
2. Design mobile-first: layouts should read as intentionally composed on phones, not squeezed desktop screens.
3. Use softness with discipline: warmer neutrals, moderate radii, and subtle shadows should make the product friendlier without becoming playful or decorative.
4. Clarify hierarchy: reduce overuse of all-caps and oversized heavy headings so important actions and information are easier to scan.
5. Keep customer trust visible: contact, credibility, and status information should stay prominent, but with calmer presentation.
