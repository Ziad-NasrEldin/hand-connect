App: Herafy

Bundle context observed: iOS Capacitor app

## Confirmed benefits

1. FIND TRUSTED HOME PROS
   - First screenshot / primary value proposition.
   - Targets customers looking for reliable home-service providers.

2. SEARCH BY SERVICE & AREA
   - Demonstrates filtering by profession and neighborhood.
   - Shows the app helps users narrow down relevant providers quickly.

3. CHECK PROFILES & REVIEWS
   - Builds trust through provider details, photos, service areas, ratings, and reviews.

4. CONTACT DIRECTLY ON WHATSAPP
   - Highlights fast conversion from discovery to direct contact.

5. JOIN AS A VERIFIED PROVIDER
   - Provider-side acquisition screenshot for craftspeople/service providers who want visibility.

## Target audience

Primary: customers in Cairo looking for verified home-service providers.
Secondary: service providers/craftspeople who want to join as verified providers.

## App context from code

Herafy is an Arabic/English home-services marketplace. Users can search providers by profession and area, view provider profiles with photos/bio/service areas/reviews, and contact via WhatsApp or in-app messaging. Providers can join/register and manage visibility/profile presence.
